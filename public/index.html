VERIFIED INFORMATION

Sources & methodology

Catalogue limits come from linked manufacturer specifications. Forecast data comes directly from Open-Meteo for your selected German location and is retrieved on each new assessment. This is not an official manufacturer or Plancraft product.

What this tool cannot prove

Actual wall temperature, moisture, primer compatibility, dew, rain resistance or curing completion cannot be established from air forecasts alone. Recoating reference times are not guarantees of rainproofing. An unknown manufacturer value is not treated as a safe value.
